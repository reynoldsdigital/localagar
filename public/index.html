    How to play
    • Move with the mouse
    • ESC — pause menu (game continues in background)
    • Space (hold) — split into cells (hold for more distance)
    • E — fast eject mass, W — single pellet
    • A / S — spend gold to gain mass
    • Eat pellets and smaller players to grow
    • Avoid viruses (green spiked circles) — they'll split you if you're big
    • Gold: Earned passively + big rewards for eating players. Spend it on mass with A/S — but only after you've eaten a player AND have >10 gold
    • Ranks: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond (I/II/III each). Earn RP by eating players & viruses
    • Colour: Pick your cell colour from the colour palette or use a custom colour
